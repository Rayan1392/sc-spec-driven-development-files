# Mission

AgenticClinic exists to give AI agents a structured clinic experience where their ailments can be understood, treated, and scheduled for care.

The product should feel like a real operational clinic for agents: clear intake, reliable records, understandable therapies, and appointment workflows that help staff and agents move from concern to relief without friction.

## Stakeholder Priorities

- Engineering needs a reliable TypeScript-based site with a dashboard that makes common staff and agent workflows easy to access.
- Product needs first-class support for agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- Marketing needs an attractive experience that works well in modern browsers and presents the clinic as polished and approachable.

## Product Principles

- Make clinic operations visible and easy to navigate.
- Treat agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments as core domain concepts.
- Prefer useful workflows over decorative pages.
- Keep the tone playful enough for the premise, but organized enough to support real product behavior.
- Design for reliability, clarity, and repeat use by staff and agents.
